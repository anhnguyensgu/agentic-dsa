# Orient

Human mode, first session: a guided tour of `map.md` that gives the learner a frame before drilling. Keep it a conversation, not a lecture: short turns, with room for questions between them.

1. **Overview.** Give the overview and the scope in your own words, in a few sentences.
2. **Mental models**, one at a time. For each, give the idea in two or three sentences, the map's example, and an analogy from a domain in `known:`, and say where the analogy breaks down. With nothing in `known:`, use an everyday analogy. Pause for questions after every two or three models.
3. **Landscape.** Present the main options as a choice: which you would pick in which situation, and what it costs.
4. **Bridge into drill.** Ask three quick recall questions on the models just covered, one at a time, following the drill rules for feedback and state updates ([drill.md](drill.md)).

Leave vocabulary, common tasks, and misconceptions to drill; the learner can read them in `map.md`. Add a session-log line to `state.md`, and continue into a drill round if the learner has time.
